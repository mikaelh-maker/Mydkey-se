Infrastructure and cloud for businesses

I build and develop cloud-based infrastructure that is easy to manage and secure from the start, for businesses in Stockholm and across Sweden.

Get in touch Take the security check

What I help with

  • Virtual data centres and cloud-based work environments
  • Server, client and network operations
  • Linux, containers and Kubernetes
  • Microsoft 365 and other platforms, based on what suits the business
  • Documented solutions that others can take over

How it works

  1. The starting point is what the business needs, not a particular platform.
  2. The solution is secure from the start and shaped by value, cost and risk.
  3. Everything is documented so it can be managed and taken over by others.

Frequently asked questions

Should we move everything to the cloud?

Not necessarily. It depends on what you have, what it costs and what requirements apply. I help weigh value against cost and risk and recommend what suits the business.

Do you only work with Microsoft?

No. I work with Microsoft 365 and other platforms, as well as Linux, containers and Kubernetes, based on what fits.

Will we become dependent on you?

No. Solutions are documented so that others can take over. You should never depend on a single person to understand your own IT.
