Frequently asked questions

Which computers should we buy?

It depends on how you work. A short needs analysis gives you the right level of equipment, neither oversized nor underpowered.

How do we make sure all computers are up to date?

With central device management, updates, encryption and settings are handled for all devices, without every employee having to remember.

What do we do with old computers and phones?

They should be decommissioned securely so that no company data remains. That is part of the lifecycle I help with.
