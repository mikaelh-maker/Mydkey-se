Frequently asked questions

What is DMARC, and does our company need it?

SPF, DKIM and DMARC are settings for your email domain that make it harder for others to send email in your name. Every company that uses email benefits from them, and they can be checked in a few minutes.

Where should we start if we have done nothing at all?

Start with multi-factor authentication for email and administrators, a tested backup and a procedure for confirming payments by phone. The security check on the home page gives you a first picture in two minutes.

Can you help if we have already been breached?

Yes. I have handled real security incidents and can help limit the damage, restore operations and put procedures in place so it does not happen again.
