Video surveillance for businesses

I help businesses in Stockholm and across Sweden set up camera systems that provide real security: the right cameras in the right places, securely connected and in line with the rules.

Contact me Take the security check

What I help with

A camera system should deter, provide evidence when something happens and be reliable day and night. At the same time, connected cameras are one of the most common weak spots in a business network. I build video surveillance with the same security mindset as the rest of the IT environment, so the system protects you instead of opening a new door.

  • Needs analysis and placement: what should be seen, where and why
  • Choice of cameras, recording and storage, on site or in the cloud
  • Cameras on a separate, isolated network so they never become a way in
  • Secure access from phone and computer with personal logins
  • GDPR and Swedish camera surveillance law: purpose, signage and retention

How it works

  1. Site visit and needs analysis: what needs protecting, lighting, network and who needs to see the footage.
  2. A plain-language proposal with camera placement, equipment, storage and price, and what applies for GDPR and signage.
  3. Installation, configuration and a walkthrough with you, plus a follow-up to make sure everything works as it should.

Frequently asked questions

Do we need a permit to install cameras?

Most businesses in Sweden do not need a permit to monitor their own premises, but the surveillance must comply with GDPR. That means a clear purpose, a balancing of interests, signage and limited retention. I help you get it right from the start.

Can the cameras become a security risk?

Yes, if they are set up badly. Cameras with default passwords and open internet access are a common way in for attackers. I put the cameras on their own network, change passwords, keep the software updated and provide secure remote access.

Can we view the footage on our phones?

Yes. You can watch live and play back recordings on your phone or computer, with personal logins so you can see who has viewed what.

Should recordings be stored on site or in the cloud?

It depends on the business. Local recording is fast and has no ongoing cloud cost, while cloud storage survives even if the equipment is stolen. A combination is often best, and I explain the pros and cons before you decide.
