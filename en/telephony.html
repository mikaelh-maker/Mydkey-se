Service

Telephony and phone systems for businesses

Telephony, subscriptions and phone systems as part of the rest of your IT, not as an island of their own. For businesses in Stockholm and across Sweden.

Get in touch Take the security check

What I help with

  • Mobile and fixed telephony and subscriptions, compared and procured
  • Installation, optimisation and support of business phone systems
  • Communication tools that connect with the rest of your IT
  • Smooth switchovers without disrupting the business

How it works

  1. A review of how you make and receive calls today, and what your subscriptions cost.
  2. A comparison of alternatives and help with procurement.
  3. A planned switchover without downtime, and support for the phone system afterwards.

Frequently asked questions

Can we switch operator or phone system without the phones going down?

Yes, with planning. Number porting, settings and call forwarding are prepared in advance, so the switch happens without disrupting the business.

How does telephony connect with the rest of our IT?

Phone systems, mobiles and communication tools affect both the workplace and security, for example sign-in and who has access. Planning them together makes everything easier to manage and more secure.

Do you only help with procurement, or afterwards too?

Both. I help with comparison and procurement, and then with installation, optimisation and support of the phone system.
