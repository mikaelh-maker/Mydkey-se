Privacy

This page briefly describes how Mydkey AB handles personal data on mydkey.se.

What the site collects

The site uses no cookies and no web analytics. I do not track visitors.

The security check on the home page is calculated in your browser. Your answers are not sent to me or anyone else, and they are not stored. Only if you choose to send the result by email will I see what you write.

News feed

The news band on the home page shows headlines and links to articles from independent news sources and public agencies. The headlines are fetched automatically and stored on this site, so your browser does not contact any other website until you click a story yourself. Mydkey is not responsible for the content of external sites.

If you contact me

If you email or call me, I process the details you provide (for example your name, contact details and your enquiry) in order to reply. The details are not used for anything else and are not passed on. They are kept for as long as needed to handle the enquiry and thereafter as required by law.

Hosting

The site is delivered by a hosting service that may record technical data, such as IP addresses, in its server logs. This takes place at the service provider and is used for operations and security.

Your rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of or deletion of your personal data. Get in touch and I will help you. You can also contact the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY).
